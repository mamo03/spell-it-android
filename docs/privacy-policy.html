Privacy Policy

Spell It! · Last updated September 28, 2026

Spell It! collects no personal data

The short version

Spell It! is a word-spelling game for kids. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information, from children or from adults. There are no user accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, and no advertising anywhere in the app. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, Spell It!:

  • Does not require or ask for a name, email address, birthdate, or any other personal detail.
  • Does not create user accounts or profiles.
  • Does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software of any kind.
  • Does not display advertising and does not use advertising IDs.
  • Does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or any other sensitive device data.

The only thing the app remembers is your last-chosen language (English, Danish, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Dutch, or Arabic). This single preference is saved using your device's local browser storage (localStorage), stays only on your device, and is never transmitted anywhere.

Text-to-speech

Spell It! reads words and sentences aloud using your device's own built-in text-to-speech engine (Android's native TextToSpeech service). This happens on-device; the app itself does not send any text to a server to be read aloud. Depending on the text-to-speech engine and language voices installed on your device, some processing may be handled by your device's operating system rather than the app itself, subject to your device manufacturer's own settings.

Network access

Spell It! requests internet access for exactly one purpose: loading its decorative lettering (the "Baloo 2" and "Baloo Bhaijaan 2" webfonts) from Google Fonts when a connection is available. No personal or gameplay data is included in this request. If there is no internet connection, the app works fully offline and simply falls back to your device's default system font — nothing else in the app requires or uses the internet.

Children's privacy

Spell It! is designed to be safe and appropriate for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children either, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. There is no in-app purchasing, no chat or messaging functionality, and no links that lead a child out of the app.

Data sharing

Since Spell It! does not collect any data, it has no data to share, sell, or disclose to third parties.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date above.
